# Community conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome questions,
review technical claims with evidence, and acknowledge mistakes and uncertainty.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sharing private information and
deliberate misrepresentation of benchmark results are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation after
review. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through
the contact channel they publish on the hosted repository. Do not publish the
private details of another participant in an issue.
